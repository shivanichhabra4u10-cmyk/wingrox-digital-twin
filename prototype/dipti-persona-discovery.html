00 — How to read this

Three levels of certainty. Nothing more.

Public activity can raise good questions. It cannot prove who someone is. Every claim in this document carries one of three labels, and the filter above lets you read only the level you trust.

Observed

There is public evidence

Visible activity, published writing, or indexed records support this. It still needs interpretation, but the fact itself is reasonably solid.

Hypothesis

A pattern that may be real

Signals point somewhere, but they do not prove it. Treat every one of these as a question for the immersion call, never as a finding.

Unknown

No basis for a view

Money, family, risk appetite, fears, ambition. These must come from Dipti directly. Naming them as unknown is part of the work.

Persona discovery should never tell Dipti “this is who you are.” It should help her answer: “which parts of who I already am should become more important next?”The governing principle
What this document does
  • Organises public signals into patterns worth testing
  • Separates evidence from guesswork, out loud
  • Supplies the questions for a 60–90 minute immersion call
  • Offers experiments that produce evidence, not more theory

What it must not do
  • Assume anything about family, religion, health or finances
  • Assign a personality type from social media

The 90-day plan

Days 1–30 · Discover

Five audits

  • Reflection: proudest of, energised by, drained by, would stop, want more time for, want to be known for, keep postponing
  • Life audit: for work, money, relationships, health, time, creativity, learning — keep / change / remove / explore
  • Energy audit: mark four weeks of activities from +2 to −2, then find the pattern
  • Relationship audit: who inspires, supports, teaches, drains — then name the 20 that matter
  • Career audit: good at & enjoy (deepen) · good at, no longer enjoy (reduce) · want to learn (build) · neither (stop)
Days 31–60 · Experiment

Two tests: one professional, one personal

  • Interview test: three people she respects, on their decisions, failures and lessons
  • Writing test: four original posts, one clear idea each, in her own voice
  • Advisory test: own the diagnosis, questions, options and recommendation for one real challenge
  • Moderation test: moderate one discussion or panel
  • Creative test: a recording, an artist collaboration, a small music evening, or a restarted project
Days 61–90 · Evaluate

Score, then narrow

Rate every experiment 1–10 on energy, enjoyment, ability, learning, external response, demand, ownership, sustainability and future interest.

Then reduce the future map to a maximum of two pathways. The aim is to remove noise, not to decide a career.

Key question after the creative test: did this feel like an important part of me returning, or simply a pleasant activity?

06 — Behaviour, decisions, leadership

How she may operate — and where each strength turns on itself

Not a psychometric assessment. Public information can suggest patterns; it cannot confirm traits. Every item here is a behaviour to validate through real examples, never a label to assign.

The likely behavioural core

Hypothesis

Observe → interpret → connect → adapt → execute

Understand the people and context. Work out what matters. Bring the right people or ideas together. Adjust to the situation. Make sure the outcome happens.

She may influence less through formal authority and more through credibility, relationships, reading the room, knowing what matters to whom, preparing well, and creating trust. Likely strongest where the work needs both human understanding and something meaningful getting done.
